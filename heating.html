Heating

Warm rooms. Your rules.

The Heating device is a thermostat in Homey, with your heat pump's heating settings right behind it. And it can listen to the sensors you already have.

The colder it gets outside, the warmer the water your heat pump sends round the house. Heat curve displacement moves the whole curve up or down. Above the cut-off, heating stops.

Heat curve, by outdoor temperatureMoved up with displacement

A real thermostat.

Heating shows up in Homey's Climate view, with the room temperature and the target on one tile.

01

See it.

Indoor and outdoor temperature, the water going out to your radiators or floor and coming back, and what your heat pump is aiming for.

02

Shape it.

Heat curve and Heat curve displacement, plus the lowest and highest water temperature your heat pump may send.

03

Switch it.

The on/off is Allow heating on your heat pump. Off means no heating, until you switch it back on.

Summer, sorted.

Two settings decide when your heat pump bothers to heat at all.

Only heat below.

Above this outdoor temperature, your heat pump stops heating. Only use immersion heater below does the same for the electric heater, so it stays out of mild days.

Measured over time.

Both go by your heat pump's average outdoor temperature, not this minute's. So a warm afternoon doesn't switch heating off on its own.

Let your rooms guide it.

Already have temperature sensors in Homey? Your heat pump can use them instead of its own. Pick one room, or average several.

Heating›Repair›Heating setup
In Homey

Pick your sensors.

Choose Use your own Homey temperature sensors. Search by name, room or device type. Pick one, or several: each counts equally.

On your heat pump

Let it listen.

Setup lists the menus: turn on the external room sensor (setup calls it BT50) in 7.5.9.2, give it your heating zone in 1.3.3 / 1.3.4, and let it control the heating.

In Homey

Verify settings.

Homey starts sending the temperature and checks that your heat pump got it. Then Save changes. It keeps going after you close Repair.

Your heat pump, still in charge.

Same thermostat. Same settings.

Your sensors only tell your heat pump how warm it is. It still decides how to heat, with your target temperature and your Smart price adaption settings.

Every 30 seconds.

The temperature is sent again even when it hasn't changed, so your heat pump never works from an old reading.

30 s

Two zones? You choose.

If your heat pump has more than one zone, Heating setup first asks Which zone should the thermostat set? Pick the zone whose room sensor controls the heating. You'll find that in menu 1.3.3. With Nibe's wireless room sensors, each one is listed with its current reading, so you can tell them apart and show the one you want on the thermostat.

Outdoor temperature, from a Flow.

Set outdoor temperature (BT1) hands your heat pump an outdoor temperature from Homey, in place of its own outdoor sensor. It's for people who want to experiment.

Set outdoor temperature (BT1): 4.5 °C

Allow it.

Turn on Outdoor temperature (BT1) in menu 7.5.9.2 on your heat pump. Modbus alone isn't enough.

Then

Keep it coming.

The card sends one reading. Your Flow has to send fresh ones regularly, even when the value hasn't changed.

And check

Look at the display.

A Flow that succeeds means your heat pump accepted the value, not that heating uses it. Check its outdoor reading follows.

Good to know.

It's warmer outside than Only heat below, but it's still heating.

The cut-off goes by the average outdoor temperature. It can be 17 °C right now while the average is still under your limit. In Manual mode, the cut-off doesn't apply.

What if a sensor stops reporting?

Every sensor you picked needs a valid reading between 5 and 40 °C. If one drops out, Homey stops sending and keeps retrying, and after 5 minutes you get a warning on the Heating device and in your timeline. It never quietly leaves a room out of the average. If it stays down, your heat pump can raise an alarm.

Can I set up sensors while pairing?

You can pick them, and they're saved. Sending starts once you've opened Heating, then Repair, and pressed Verify settings.

How do I go back to my heat pump's own sensor?

On your heat pump, make your original sensor control the zone again and turn off the external room sensor. Then choose Use your heat pump’s own temperature sensor in Heating setup. Homey checks before it stops sending.

Will it work on my heat pump?

It needs a heat pump and software that support an external room sensor. It has been confirmed on an S1155. Menus can be named or numbered differently on other models.

I use Autopilot. Are my sensors sent to my heat pump?

No. With Autopilot, your Homey sensors are only read, and Autopilot keeps that temperature at your thermostat. Room-sensor control on your heat pump stays off. Sending them as an external room sensor is for Smart price adaption or Off.

Heating setup asks about Smart mode.

That's who moves heating to cheaper hours: Autopilot, Smart price adaption or Off. It comes first, because Autopilot asks for the indoor temperature its own way, and never uses the external room sensor. See Autopilot for heating.

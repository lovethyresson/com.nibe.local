Autopilot

Warm home. Smarter hours.

Autopilot looks at prices and the weather, then heats a bit more before expensive hours and a bit less during them. Always within the comfort range you pick.

Indoor temperature rises a little before each price peak and dips a little during it, and never leaves the comfort range around your thermostat.

It speaks your heat pump's language.

No new controls inside your heat pump. Autopilot changes one thing it already listens to.

A nudge on the thermometer.

Your heat pump decides how hard to heat from the outdoor temperature it sees. Autopilot sends it a slightly different one: a touch colder before a price peak, so it heats more, and a touch milder during it, so it heats less. Your heat pump does the rest, same as always.

Away, for the holidays.

For a week or more away, not a day at work. Turn on Away and the thermostat drops to your Away temperature, and Autopilot lets the house cool to it. Turn it off and it goes back to where it was. If Autopilot runs your hot water too, the tank keeps only its safety net while you're gone.

Good to know.

How long until it does something?

It learns your house for at least five days first, and changes nothing meanwhile. Your Homey timeline tells you when it's done and starts moving heat away from expensive hours.

Does it keep my thermostat temperature?

Yes. Autopilot steers the house to your thermostat and moves heat around it within your comfort range. Change the thermostat and it follows. A small, steady offset it corrects slowly, over a few days.

Will my house get cold?

It stays within the comfort range you picked. On Comfort, that's half a degree either side of your thermostat.

Can I pause it for a while?

Set Comfort range to Off. Your heat pump then sees the real outdoor temperature, and Autopilot neither shifts heat nor steers to your thermostat, until you pick a range again.

What does it do while my heat pump is cooling?

It pauses, and Autopilot now says so. It picks up again when heating does.

How do I hand heating back to my heat pump?

Set Smart mode to Off in Heating setup, under Repair on the Heating device. Then turn off the external outdoor sensor on your heat pump, in menu 7.5.9.2. Setup reminds you.

Can I set it up while pairing?

Yes. Heating setup asks for Smart mode first, then the indoor temperature, the checks on your heat pump and Verify. Later, it's all under Repair on the Heating device.

Where can I see the plan?

In the Autopilot widget on your Homey dashboard: indoor temperature, measured and expected, for the last and next twelve hours.

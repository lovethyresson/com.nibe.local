Where the figures come from

  • The weeks come from the 15-minute Autopilot log the Homey keeps: indoor, thermostat, BT1 offset, price and power.
  • Before Autopilot is the house model run over the same day twice, once with the offsets Autopilot fed and once with their average held steady (shift-cost.ts). Weather and prices are the same on both sides.
  • The heating cost counts the slots without hot water.
  • Saved per 1 kr of spread divides a week's savings by its average daily price spread, so a week of flat prices doesn't make its range look bad.
  • You switch the range each Monday with the "Set Autopilot heating comfort range" card, or in Heating setup.

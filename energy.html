Energy

Every kWh. In its place.

Heating, Hot water, Pool and Cooling each get their own meter in Homey's Energy tab. So you can finally see what your hot water costs.

One meter. Shared out.

Your heat pump has one electricity meter, not four. But it always says what it's busy with.

Whatever's running gets the bill.

Every few seconds, Nibe Live reads how much power your heat pump is drawing and what it's working on, and books that energy to the matching device as Energy used. Standby goes to the Main device as Idle energy. Since each moment goes to exactly one device, they always add up to your heat pump's real total.

Add every job you have.

If you didn't add a Hot water device, say, the energy spent on hot water lands on Main instead. Nothing goes missing. It's counted on Main instead of on its own.

Heat out, measured.

Energy delivered is the heat each job produced. Your heat pump counts that itself, per job, so it's read straight off and not estimated.

COP, in plain words.

kWh of heat per kWh of electricity. The higher, the less your heat costs.

A COP of 3.

Three kWh of heat for every kWh you pay for.

3×

The last 30 days.

Every COP is worked out over a rolling 30-day window. A lifetime average stops moving. Thirty days follows the seasons, so you can see what a cold snap or a settings change actually did.

On Heating, Hot water, Pool, Cooling

Heating COP (30-day) and friends. How efficient each job is.

On Main

Total COP (30-day). Your whole heat pump.

Trust Total COP most.

Both halves of Total COP come straight from your heat pump's own counters. A job's COP divides measured heat by electricity Nibe Live shared out, so it carries that small estimate with it. Close, but Total COP is the one to quote.

Close to myUplink. Not identical.

Temperatures and settings match exactly. Energy is counted a little differently, on purpose.

Counts from day one

Totals start when you added the device, not when your heat pump was installed. Compare a day or a week, not the totals.

Within about 1 %

Per-job electricity, checked against your heat pump's own hourly figures on measured hot water runs.

Live, not hourly

Your heat pump only shares its per-job split an hour late. Nibe Live counts as it happens, so prices land in the right hour.

Gaps stay gaps

If Homey restarts or loses contact, the electricity used meanwhile isn't counted.

Good to know.

Why does Main show so little?

Main isn't the whole heat pump. It carries standby, plus any job you haven't added a device for. The whole heat pump is all your Nibe devices together, which is what Homey's Energy tab adds up.

Why is my COP blank?

It needs a little history first, so it's blank for a while after you add a device. It also waits until a meaningful amount of electricity has been used, so a job that never runs, like a pool you don't have, stays blank rather than showing nonsense.

No COP on Pool or Cooling?

Some models don't share the heat delivered for those jobs. Without it there's no COP, but the energy used still works.

I compared for ten minutes and it's way off.

Compare over a day. Your heat pump's own counters move in small steps and settle about an hour behind, so a short comparison can look badly wrong when both are fine.

The power right now differs from myUplink.

Both are probably right. Nibe Live reads your heat pump directly, while myUplink goes through Nibe's cloud and can lag by minutes.

My hot water COP looks far too high.

Update your heat pump's software. Older versions have been seen counting several times too much heat delivered. After updating, run Repair on each device. Since COP covers 30 days, old readings take a month to roll out.

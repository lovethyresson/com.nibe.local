Get started

Getting started

Turn on one setting on your heat pump, add Nibe Live, and Homey looks for it on your own network. It works without a cloud account, and readings arrive in seconds.

Nibe heat pump · Outdoor temperature 4.5 °C

On your heat pump

Homey talks to your heat pump directly, so your heat pump has to allow it. You turn that on with one setting on its display.

Menu 7.5.9›Modbus TCP/IP›On
On your heat pump

Update the software

Many readings only exist on recent software. Approve a waiting update in myUplink, or use a USB stick and menu 8.1. Your settings are kept.

On your heat pump

Turn on Modbus TCP

In menu 7.5.9. Without it, Homey can't find your heat pump or talk to it.

In your home

Same network

Your heat pump and your Homey Pro need to be on the same home network.

Adding it in Homey

In Homey, add a device and pick Nibe Live, then Nibe S-Series. Pairing walks you through the rest.

01

Find your heat pump

Homey searches your network and shows what it found, with the outdoor temperature so you can tell it's yours. Tap it to confirm. Nothing found? Choose Enter the IP address manually.

02

Detecting features

About half a minute of watching which values change, so the app knows what your model and accessories have.

03

Add devices

Pick the parts of your heat pump you want in Homey. The ones with live data come ticked. Then Add selected devices.

Choosing devices

Your heat pump becomes a handful of devices, one per job. Add the ones you'll use.

Devices with data come ticked

Each device in the list says Data detected or No data. No pool? Pool comes unticked. You can still add anything, and expand a device to fine-tune what it carries.

Device setup

Before the devices are created, Device setup asks a couple of things for the jobs you picked. In Hot water setup, pick your tank so the Hot water device can tell you how many litres are left. In Heating setup, pick Smart mode and which temperature guides your heating. What gets added shows what each device gets before anything is created. You'll find both setups again under Repair.

Good to know

Homey didn't find my heat pump.

Check that Modbus TCP is on in menu 7.5.9 and that Homey is on the same network. Then tap Search again, or type the IP address yourself. That way always works.

Can I keep using myUplink?

Yes. myUplink goes through NIBE's cloud and doesn't get in the way. What you can't do is connect a second Modbus app or integration at the same time. Your heat pump takes one at a time.

Some values stay blank.

Your model probably doesn't report them. Not every heat pump has every reading, and the app leaves out what yours doesn't answer. COP is the exception. It stays blank until there's enough to go on.

My heat pump's IP address changed.

The app notices and searches your network for it after a few minutes. To avoid it altogether, give your heat pump a fixed address (a DHCP reservation) in your router.

Can I add more devices later?

Yes. Add a device again and pick the same heat pump. Pairing only offers the devices you haven't added yet.

I updated my heat pump's software after pairing.

Run Repair on each device, so the app checks what your heat pump offers now.

Good to know.

Can I run Smart price adaption and Autopilot together?

Not for the same function. Two planners would fight over one compressor. When you pick Autopilot for heating, it turns off Smart price adaption for heating.

What if someone turns Smart price adaption back on?

Your heat pump wins. If it's switched on from the display or myUplink, Autopilot steps aside for that function and your Homey timeline tells you once.

Does Simulated change anything?

No. It plans and shows you the plan, and your heat pump runs exactly as before.

Why does Autopilot say Experimental?

Because it's new. You'll see the badge wherever you choose Autopilot or see what it's doing, so it's always clear which parts are fresh.

I don't see Smart price adaption.

Not every heat pump has it. When yours doesn't, the choice isn't offered.

Can heating and hot water use different choices?

Yes. Each function has its own Smart mode. Autopilot for hot water and Smart price adaption for heating is a perfectly good mix.

Heating

The Heating device

The Heating device is a thermostat in Homey, with your heat pump's heating settings right behind it. It can also use the temperature sensors you already have in Homey.

The colder it gets outdoors, the warmer the water your heat pump sends round the house. Curve offset moves the whole curve up or down. Above the cut-off, heating stops.

Heating curve, by outdoor temperatureMoved up with the offset

The thermostat

Heating shows up in Homey's Climate view, with the room temperature and the target on one tile.

01

Readings

Indoor and outdoor temperature, the water going out to your radiators or floor and coming back, and what your heat pump is aiming for.

02

Heating curve

Heating curve and Curve offset, plus the lowest and highest water temperature your heat pump may send.

03

On and off

The on/off is Allow heating on your heat pump. Off means no heating, until you switch it back on.

Heating in summer

Two settings decide when your heat pump heats.

Only heat below

Above this outdoor temperature, your heat pump stops heating. Only use immersion heater below does the same for the immersion heater, so it stays out of mild days.

Average outdoor temperature

Both go by your heat pump's average outdoor temperature, so a warm afternoon doesn't switch heating off on its own.

Using your own room sensors

Already have temperature sensors in Homey? Your heat pump can use them instead of its own. Pick one room, or average several.

Heating›Repair›Heating setup
In Homey

Pick your sensors

Choose Use your own Homey temperature sensors. Search by name, room or device type. Pick one or several. Each counts equally.

On your heat pump

Turn on the external sensor

Setup lists the menus. Turn on the external room sensor (BT50) in menu 7.5.9.2, give it your heating zone in menu 1.3.3 / 1.3.4, and let it control the heating.

In Homey

Verify settings

Homey starts sending the temperature and checks that your heat pump got it. Then Save changes. Sending continues after you close Repair.

How your heat pump uses the sensors

Your thermostat and settings

Your sensors only tell your heat pump how warm it is. It still decides how to heat, with your target temperature and your Smart Price Adaption settings.

Sent every 30 seconds

The temperature is sent again even when it hasn't changed, so your heat pump never works from an old reading.

30 s

More than one zone

If your heat pump has more than one zone, Heating setup first asks Which zone should the thermostat set? Pick the zone whose room sensor controls the heating. You'll find that in menu 1.3.3. With NIBE's wireless room sensors, each one is listed with its current reading, so you can tell them apart and show the one you want on the thermostat.

Outdoor temperature from a Flow

Set outdoor temperature BT1 to … sends your heat pump an outdoor temperature from Homey, in place of its own outdoor sensor. It's for people who want to experiment.

Set outdoor temperature BT1 to 4.5 °C
First

Turn on external BT1

Turn on Outdoor temperature (BT1) in menu 7.5.9.2 on your heat pump. Modbus alone isn't enough.

Then

Send it regularly

The card sends one reading per run. Your Flow has to repeat it every few minutes, even when the value hasn't changed.

And check

Check the display

A Flow that succeeds only means your heat pump accepted the value. Check that its outdoor reading follows.

Good to know

The outdoor temperature is above Only heat below, but it's still heating.

The cut-off goes by the average outdoor temperature. It can be 17 °C right now while the average is still under your limit. In Manual mode, the cut-off doesn't apply.

What if a sensor stops reporting?

Every sensor you picked needs a valid reading between 5 and 40 °C. If one drops out, Homey stops sending and keeps retrying, and after 5 minutes you get a warning on the Heating device and in your Timeline. Homey never drops a room from the average without telling you. If it stays down, your heat pump can raise an alarm.

Can I set up sensors while pairing?

You can pick them, and they're saved. Sending starts once you've opened Heating, then Repair, and pressed Verify settings.

How do I go back to my heat pump's own sensor?

On your heat pump, make your original sensor control the zone again and turn off the external room sensor. Then choose Use your heat pump’s own temperature sensor in Heating setup. Homey checks before it stops sending.

Will it work on my heat pump?

It needs a heat pump and software that support an external room sensor. It has been confirmed on an S1155. Menus can be named or numbered differently on other models.

I use Autopilot. Are my sensors sent to my heat pump?

Only if your heat pump has an external room sensor (BT50) turned on. The room temperature is then sent as BT50 to prevent sensor alarms. Room-sensor control must be off, so your heat pump only shows the temperature, and Autopilot keeps it at your thermostat.

Heating setup asks about Smart mode.

Smart mode decides who moves heating to cheaper hours: Autopilot, Smart Price Adaption or Off. It comes first, because Autopilot asks for the indoor temperature its own way, and never uses the external room sensor. See Autopilot for heating.

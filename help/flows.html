Flows

Your home. Your rules.

Every Nibe Live device brings its own Flow cards, so your heat pump can react to the rest of your home. And the rest of your home can react to it.

Cards that say what they do.

No long lists to scroll through. Each card is named after the setting it changes, on the device it belongs to.

One card per setting.

Change a setting, or check one, in plain words. You'll find them on the Heating, Hot water and Main devices, wherever the setting lives.

Old Flows keep running.

Cards from earlier versions that picked a setting from a long list still work in your existing Flows. They're only hidden when you build new ones.

Why does the calendar Flow set a type every night?

A day type you set holds until something changes it. Setting it every night, both ways, keeps it in step with your calendar. Where nothing was ever set, Saturday and Sunday count as days off on their own.

Will the nightly Flow end Away?

No. While you're away, Set day type only remembers which day to go back to. Away ends when you turn it off.

I don't see the Autopilot cards.

The Autopilot heating cards appear on the Heating device once Autopilot or Simulation runs heating. Set day type is on Main once Autopilot or Simulation runs hot water. The hot water cards are on Hot water, and need a tank picked in Hot water setup.

Does A hot water charge started fire for charges Autopilot didn't plan?

Yes. It fires for every charge, and tells you whether Autopilot started it.

A Flow turns on More hot water on a schedule.

With Autopilot on for hot water, that starts charges it didn't plan. Setup lists any Flows like that, so you can turn them off or keep them for notifications.

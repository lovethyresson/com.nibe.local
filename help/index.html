Nibe Live

Help

Nibe Live connects to your NIBE heat pump directly over your own network, without a cloud account. Every job it does gets its own device, its own meter and its own controls.

Local

It keeps working when myUplink is down or your internet is out. Readings arrive in seconds.

Split

Heating, hot water, pool and cooling each become their own device, so you can automate one without touching the rest.

Autopilot

Autopilot heats your home and your hot water in the cheapest hours, and tells you why.

Devices

One heat pump. Several devices.

Your heat pump does several jobs, and you rarely want to handle them together. So each job gets its own device in Homey, with its own controls and its own energy meter.

MainHeatingHot waterPoolCoolingSolar

A tile for every job.

Add the ones your heat pump actually does. Pairing ticks those it found data for.

Main.

The heat pump itself. What it's doing right now, the operating mode, alarms in plain words instead of codes, fans and ventilation if you have them, and runtime stats. It also holds the master switch for Smart price adaption and the Total COP.

Up to six.

From one heat pump. Main is always on, since your heat pump has no off switch for the whole unit.

Heating.

A thermostat in Homey's Climate view: room temperature and target on one tile. The heat curve, supply temperatures and when heating stops for the summer live here too. More on Heating.

Hot water.

How much hot water you want, the temperatures behind it, More hot water for a one-off boost, and how many litres are left in the tank. More on Hot water.

Pool.

A thermostat for your pool water, if you have the POOL 40 accessory.

Cooling.

Whether cooling is allowed, and the outdoor temperature that starts it.

Solar.

With the EME 20 accessory, what your panels make shows up in Homey Energy as production.

Each one counts for itself.

Heating, Hot water, Pool and Cooling each carry the same three numbers, so you can finally see what your hot water costs.

01

Energy used.

The electricity that job used, worked out from what your heat pump was doing at the time. Time spent idle goes to Main.

02

Energy delivered.

The heat that came out, read straight from your heat pump's own counters.

03

COP.

Heat out for each unit of electricity in, over the last 30 days. Higher is better.

On means on.

The on/off on Heating, Hot water, Pool and Cooling is a real control. Turning the Heating device off turns heating off on your heat pump, and back on again when you switch it on. Flows can do the same.

Change a device

Use Repair.

Open the device and choose Repair. It checks your heat pump again, then lets you turn features on or off. What changes shows the result before you save.

Remove a device

Delete it.

Like any Homey device. Its energy then counts on Main, so the total still adds up.

Good to know.

Do I need all of them?

No. Add the ones you'll use. But add every job you want metered: skip Hot water, and the energy your heat pump spends on hot water shows up on Main instead.

Why does Main show so little energy?

Main carries what's left over: standby, plus any job you haven't added a device for. Your heat pump's real total is all your Nibe devices added together, which is what Homey Energy shows.

Where are the fan and ventilation settings?

On Main. Ventilation serves heating and hot water alike, so it sits with the other whole-heat-pump settings.

My Hot water device has no on/off.

Some models don't offer that switch to apps, including the S2125, S320, S325, S330, S332 and S735. Everything else on the device still works.

What happens to history when I turn a feature off?

Turning a feature off in Repair removes its readings from the device, and their Insights history goes with them.

Why can't I turn some readings off?

While Autopilot runs, the readings and settings it acts on stay on their device, whatever you ticked. Set Smart mode to something else and your own choice comes back.

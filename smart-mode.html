Smart mode

Smart mode

Smart mode decides who moves heating and hot water to cheaper hours. One choice per function, and only one planner runs at a time.

The choices

Pairing asks for it, and you can change it any time.

Off

Nothing is moved to cheaper hours. Heating follows your heating curve, and hot water follows your heat pump's own settings.

Smart Price Adaption

NIBE's own price feature, running inside your heat pump. It gets prices through myUplink and works even with Homey switched off. You set how strongly the price may push each job around.

Autopilot

The app's own planner. It runs in Nibe Live, learns your house and your routine, reads the weather forecast and your Homey Energy prices, and plans around them.

Which one to choose

Each choice has different strengths. It depends on what you want.

Autopilot

Learns your home

Uses the weather forecast and your full electricity price, which includes more than the spot price. Can heat hot water with your own solar power. Needs Homey running, and a few days to learn first.

Smart Price Adaption

Works from day one

Runs in your heat pump, so it keeps going with Homey off. Covers heating, hot water, pool and cooling. Plans on the spot price only.

Off

Ignores prices

Your heat pump heats when it needs to, whatever the price.

Where to change it

Pairing asks in Heating setup and Hot water setup. To change it later, open that device, then Repair. Smart mode is in the same setup.

Heating›Repair›Heating setup
Step 1

Pick a mode

For Autopilot, pick Live or Simulated. Smart Price Adaption only shows up if your heat pump has it.

Step 2

Follow the checks

For Autopilot, setup lists what it needs and what to set on your heat pump.

Step 3

See what changes

Setup ends on What gets added, or What changes in Repair. It lists every reading and setting that's added or removed, and why.

Good to know

Can I run Smart Price Adaption and Autopilot together?

Not for the same function. Two planners would fight over one compressor. When you pick Autopilot for heating, it turns off Smart Price Adaption for heating.

What if someone turns Smart Price Adaption back on?

Your heat pump's setting takes priority. If it's switched on from the display or myUplink, Autopilot steps aside for that function and your Homey Timeline tells you once.

Does Simulated change anything?

No. Simulated previews the plan without making changes to your heat pump.

Why does Autopilot say Experimental?

Autopilot is new. The badge shows wherever you choose Autopilot or see what it's doing.

I don't see Smart Price Adaption.

Not every heat pump has it. When yours doesn't, the choice isn't offered.

Can heating and hot water use different choices?

Yes. Each function has its own Smart mode. Autopilot for hot water and Smart Price Adaption for heating is a good combination.
